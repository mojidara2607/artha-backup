Privacy policy

Artha backup is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

It asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With it, the app can see, create and delete only the files it created itself (a folder called "Artha backups" and the backup files in it). It cannot see or change any other file in the Google Drive.

What it does with that access

  • Once a day it uploads a backup of the program's own data from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive.
  • It deletes its own older backup files so the Drive does not fill up.

Sharing

Nothing is sent to anyone else. There is no server, no analytics and no advertising. The sign-in token stays encrypted on the owner's computer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app's settings ("Disconnect") or from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
